# Use real-time reporting use-real-time-reporting

Last update: May 13, 2026
- Topics:
- [Real-time Reporting](#)

CREATED FOR:

- User

To use real-time reporting, enable the **Real-time refresh** toggle on any of the following panels in your Workspace project:

- [Blank panel](/en/docs/analytics-platform/using/cja-workspace/panels/blank-panel)
- [Freeform](/en/docs/analytics-platform/using/cja-workspace/panels/freeform-panel)
- [Attribution](/en/docs/analytics-platform/using/cja-workspace/panels/attribution)
- [Next or previous item](/en/docs/analytics-platform/using/cja-workspace/panels/next-previous)

You see a message with the timestamp of the most recent refresh of the data. For example: *Last refreshed at 07:55 pm*.

Select the real-time period that you want to report on from the drop-down menu. Available options are:

- Last 15 minutes
- Last 30 minutes
- Last hour
- Last 8 hours
- Last 24 hours

All visualizations in the panel are now updated every minute for a maximum of 30 minutes while the browser tab with the real-time refresh enabled panel is active.

As an example, see below a snapshot of a **Real-time reporting panel** that refreshes the **Total Revenue / Hour** bar visualization and **Total Revenue / Hour** freeform table as time moves from *06:26pm* to *06:27 pm*.

After 30 minutes, or as soon as the browser tab becomes inactive, the **Real-time refresh** toggle is disabled automatically and real-time updates are stopped.

As soon as the Real-time refresh toggle is disabled, the panel (and all visualizations within) return to [use the standard reporting data and features from Customer Journey Analytics](/en/docs/analytics-platform/using/cja-components/real-time-reporting/real-time#how-it-works).
