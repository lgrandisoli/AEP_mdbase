Last update: June 5, 2026
- Topics:

- [Integrations](#)

This page illustrates how to tie Customer Journey Analytics (CJA) and Quantum Metric together, to get the most out of both products working together. The use cases covered include:

- [Tie Quantum Metric session replays to data in Customer Journey Analytics](/en/docs/analytics-platform/using/cja-usecases/third-party/qm/tie-session-replays) to better understand “the why” behind the “what”.
- [Add Quantum Metric error and friction events](/en/docs/analytics-platform/using/cja-usecases/third-party/qm/friction-events), such as page load slowness and error, rage clicks and more to CJA behaviorial data to add depth to analysis.
- Better understand page-level engagement by [linking Quantum Metric heatmapping with CJA data](/en/docs/analytics-platform/using/cja-usecases/third-party/qm/heatmap).
