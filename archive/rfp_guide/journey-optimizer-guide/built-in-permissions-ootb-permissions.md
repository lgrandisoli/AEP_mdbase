Last update: May 8, 2026
- Topics:
- [Access Management](#)

CREATED FOR:

Built-in permissions represent the different permissions that can be assigned to a **Role** to fine-tune your user access to Journey Optimizer. High-level permissions encompass low-level permissions, detailed on [this page](/en/docs/journey-optimizer/using/access-control/high-low-permissions).
