# Manage consent section-overview

Last update: May 8, 2026
Managing consent is key to building trust with your audience while ensuring compliance with privacy regulations such as GDPR. Consent in Adobe Journey Optimizer works alongside [privacy management](/en/docs/journey-optimizer/using/privacy/get-started-privacy)—including [data requests](/en/docs/journey-optimizer/using/privacy/requests) and [audit logs](/en/docs/journey-optimizer/using/privacy/audit-logs)—and [Data Governance](/en/docs/journey-optimizer/using/privacy/action-privacy) policies that protect sensitive fields from being exported to third-party systems.

You can handle consent in three ways: **opt-out preferences** for unsubscriptions across email, SMS, and push; **consent policies** applied to your campaigns and journeys to control data usage and marketing actions; and **preference management** to target customers based on the subscription topics and channels they choose. The topics below guide you through each step.
